our story

A small café,
named after a dog

Goki's Café sits on Barndesteeg, a quiet lane a few steps from Nieuwmarkt in the old centre of Amsterdam. It takes its name from Goki, the shiba inu who, most days, is somewhere in the room keeping an eye on things.

the kitchen

Built on soufflé pancakes

The café made its name on Japanese soufflé pancakes: whipped, folded and cooked to order so they come out tall and cloud-soft. They take time, and there are only so many each day, so on a busy weekend they sell out. It is worth the wait.

Around the pancakes there is a full brunch kitchen. A signature French toast with whipped ricotta and berries, sourdough plates, benedicts and bigger specials. And from the counter, specialty coffee, matcha and hojicha, hot or iced, alongside clouds, blends and fruity refreshers.

The room itself is part of it. Small and warm: oak, cream tile, hanging plants and string lights, with a handful of seats. It fills up quickly, and that is rather the point.

Fluffy pancakes, proper coffee, and one shiba who thinks he owns the place.

the dog in question

Goki, the shiba
in charge

Goki is a real, flesh-and-fur shiba inu, and the café carries his name. He is a regular fixture of the room, so if you are not a dog person it is worth knowing he is usually around. If you are, he might just come and say hello.
